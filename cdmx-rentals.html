A clearer rental search

Mexico City rentals move fast, and many of the best opportunities never feel as organized as they should. Prices shift, photos can be incomplete, and the difference between two streets can completely change the experience of living there.

Hola Homes filters the noise and helps you focus on apartments that match your budget, timing, lifestyle, commute, pets, parking needs, and preferred neighborhood feel.

Rental support can include

  • Curated apartment options instead of endless listing links.
  • Condesa, Roma Norte, Juárez, Roma Sur, Polanco, and central CDMX neighborhood guidance.
  • Showings coordination and local context before you apply or sign.
  • Lease questions, negotiation support, and practical move-in considerations.
  • Bilingual support for international renters and local clients.
