Condesa vs Roma Norte: where should you live?

They sit side by side, share the same cafes-and-trees reputation, and most people shortlist both. Here is how they actually differ day to day, from Susana Cruz, born and raised in Condesa.

The short answer

Choose Condesa if you want green, calm-ish streets, two big parks on your doorstep and an easy daily routine. Choose Roma Norte if you want restaurants, galleries and nightlife a few steps away, and you like a bit more energy around you. Many people are happy in either; the right street matters more than the name.

How Condesa feels

Condesa is built around Parque México and Parque España, with the oval of Avenida Amsterdam running through it. Wide, tree-lined medians, Art Deco buildings and dog walkers at every hour give it a softer, residential rhythm. It is very walkable and works well as a first home in the city.

It is also in high demand. Well-presented apartments near the parks and Amsterdam move quickly, so it pays to have your documents ready before you start visiting.

How Roma Norte feels

Roma Norte sits just across Avenida Insurgentes. It has some of the city's best-known restaurants, bars, galleries and independent shops, around plazas like Río de Janeiro and Luis Cabrera and along Álvaro Obregón. The architecture mixes early-1900s mansions, classic apartment buildings and newer developments.

It is livelier, especially at night and on weekends. Some blocks are very quiet; others sit next to a bar strip. Visit the same street in the evening before you commit.

Side by side

  • Pace: Condesa is greener and calmer; Roma Norte is busier and more social.
  • Green space: Condesa has Parque México and Parque España; Roma Norte has smaller plazas and tree-lined avenues.
  • Food and nightlife: both are excellent; Roma Norte has more of it, and later.
  • Noise: varies street by street in both. In Roma Norte, check for bars and weekend crowds nearby.
  • Buildings: both have many older buildings. Ask about structural reviews, maintenance and water pressure before you sign or buy.
  • Rents: similar. Well-located apartments in both commonly range from about 20,000 to 70,000 pesos a month, depending on size, building, light and furnishing.

Who each one suits

  • Condesa: families, dog owners, people working from home, and anyone who wants a park run before breakfast.
  • Roma Norte: people who eat out often, enjoy nightlife and galleries, and like a creative, busy street life.
  • Can't decide? The streets near the border between the two, around Insurgentes and Álvaro Obregón, give you easy access to both.

Before you choose

  • Walk your shortlisted streets at night and on a weekend.
  • Check light, noise and building condition in person or on a live video call.
  • Ask what maintenance fees cover and who pays them.
