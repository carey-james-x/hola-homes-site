Why clients choose Roma Sur

Roma Sur can be a smart fit for people who want charm, access, and neighborhood life without the same intensity or pricing pressure often found in the busiest parts of Roma Norte and Condesa.

What to watch for

  • Inventory varies widely between classic apartments and more practical buildings.
  • Exact location matters for walkability, transit, and access to restaurants or parks.
  • Some streets feel quiet and residential while others feel more transitional.
  • It can offer good value when Condesa or Roma Norte pricing gets tight.
