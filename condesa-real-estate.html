Why clients choose Condesa

Condesa is polished, green, social, and easy to live in. It works well for people who want parks nearby, strong cafe culture, walkable streets, and a softer landing in Mexico City without feeling disconnected from the energy of Roma Norte and central CDMX.

What to watch for

  • High demand and fast-moving rental inventory.
  • Building age, maintenance, noise, and street-by-street differences.
  • Pricing that can shift quickly depending on furniture, lease terms, and location.
  • Strong competition for well-presented apartments near Parque México and Avenida Amsterdam.
